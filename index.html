ABOUT NEXORA

Learning designed for the real world.

Nexora Global Academy (NGA) is a global learning and development platform dedicated to empowering individuals through education, innovation, leadership, financial intelligence, entrepreneurship and digital skills.

The Academy is founded on the belief that knowledge should be accessible to everyone regardless of nationality, religion, gender, background or economic status.

Through training, mentorship, research and practical learning opportunities, Nexora Global Academy aims to equip people with the skills and mindset needed to thrive in the modern world.

OUR MISSION

Education that creates opportunities.

To provide accessible, practical and transformative education that enables people to develop valuable skills, build sustainable careers, achieve financial growth and contribute positively to society.

OUR VISION

To become a world-leading academy that empowers individuals with knowledge, skills and opportunities to create meaningful impact and achieve lifelong success.

FOUNDER & EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR

Abdullahi Haadi O.

Abdullahi Haadi O. is the Founder and Executive Director of Nexora Global Academy, a learning and development platform created to empower individuals with practical knowledge, valuable skills and opportunities for meaningful growth.

His vision for Nexora Global Academy is centred on building an environment where people can learn, develop their abilities, discover opportunities and prepare themselves for the demands of a rapidly changing world.

Through Nexora, the goal is to bring together education, technology, financial intelligence, entrepreneurship, leadership and personal development in a way that is practical, accessible and relevant to everyday life.
